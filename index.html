The story

Betrayed at sea. Left for dead.

Caribbean, 1717. Aurelius Stalward is a deckhand aboard the Queen Anne’s Revenge, and the closest thing Blackbeard has to a son.

Then a ship flying their own colours comes alongside, and a dead man walks up the gangplank. François l’Olonnais was hanged by the Spanish. Torn apart in Darién. Buried a dozen times in a dozen stories. He hasn’t aged a day, and he has come for the Asure Chart.

By morning the Revenge is gone, and her crew with her. Aurelius has one reason left to live.

“If anything happens to him, I will hunt you down. Every port. Every sea.”

The sea chart

One island. Every kind of trouble.

The Isle curls around a sheltered bay like a hook in the sea. Its harbour town crowds the beach, its palms climb the ridge, and something old watches from the peak. Select a mark on the chart.

Chart of the IsleSurveyed in-engine, scale unknown
A

The Cove

Beach cabins and jetties crowd the inner curve of the island. Dock your ship, sell your plunder, and find out who in town is paying for what.

Shallow water, safe anchorage, few honest men.

B

Skull Peak

Past the palms and up the stone stair, a mountain carved into a skull keeps watch over the bay. Light burns behind its eyes. Sailors don't agree on why.

Climb at your own risk. Bring a lantern.

C

The Tavern

Sun-split walls, loose floorboards and a stove still warm. Rum flows, rumours spread, and every crew in the Cove passes through that door sooner or later.

Mind the floor. Not every board is nailed down.

Gameplay

Articles of Agreement

Set down for every hand who sails aboard the Infinite Asure

  1. Article the First

    Sail free

    Take the helm and set your own course across open water, reading the wind and the horizon.

  2. Article the Second

    Fight at sea

    Trade broadsides with rival ships, then close in, throw the hooks and board.

  3. Article the Third

    Go ashore

    Run the jetties, climb the cliffs and slip through the Cove on foot, where the ship can't follow.

  4. Article the Fourth

    Chase the Asure

    Follow rumours of the Asure Chart up Skull Peak and into the island's hidden places, toward waters no map will show.

  5. Article the Fifth

    Live the life

    Drink in the taverns, spend your share, and earn a name the whole harbour knows.

  6. Article the Sixth

    Never stop

    Beyond the reef the blue goes on. There is always another island past the next one.
